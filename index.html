Who We Are

We are an integrated sales, advertising and marketing agency that's not only creative but also professionals who will work and deliver our best services with a big network of local media across Indonesia.

Vision

Becoming a leading professional advertising and marketing agency, with high level of quality and creativity, bringing up new ideas especially in the digital era.

Mission

Helping customers and clients to achieve their targets and goals, with online and offline innovation, including collaboration with other media all over Indonesia.

WORK

What We Do

Digital Marketing Strategy

We have many ideas and solutions of some efficient, effective and economical ways to build awareness for your brand. And it's not limited to digital campaign only, but may also include offline events. Just tell us your targets and goals.

Digital Media Buying

For Google ads or any programmatic needs, as well as Facebook, Instagram or other social media ads, we will always offer you the best price. What more is that we can also offer you our diverse online portals and our big network of local media to multiply the reach of your ads.

KOL & Influencer Management

Finding and managing the right KOL or influencers for your brand is another service we can provide. We don't just know important people, but also ideas to connect your brand to the market through them.

Website & Mobile Site Development

Digital media is our group's core business, and with this we can assure you we're more than capable of being a website and mobile site planner and developer. As been recognised, some of our websites have even reach the top list in Indonesia already.

Online Application Development

Online application is also another thing we can offer, starting from polling and quiz, simple games, to more sophisticated applications. We can develop them for anything you need.

E-CRM & Point of Sales Integration

Of course, we also offer Customer Relationship Management (CRM) as customer is always the most important aspect in business. Trust us, our digital service for e-CRM & Point of Sales Integration will connect your brand more to your customers.
